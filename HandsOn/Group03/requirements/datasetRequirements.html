Dataset Requirements Analysis

Group: Group 03

Primary Datasets: Madrid Acoustic Pollution (Daily Measurements (215885) & Monitoring Stations (211346))

Portal: datos.madrid.es (Ayuntamiento de Madrid)

R1. Smart Cities Domain Mandatory

Satisfied

The datasets monitor environmental noise pollution (acoustic quality) collected by urban sensor networks throughout the city of Madrid. Noise management, acoustic zoning, and monitoring citizen environmental health are essential pillars of modern smart city governance.

R2. Available as CSV Mandatory

Satisfied

Both source files are directly published and downloadable in CSV format:

  • Measurements: Ruido_diario_acumulado.csv — contains columns: Estación;Año;Mes;Día;Periodo;LAeq;L1;L10;L50;L90;L99. The Periodo field categorizes readings into D (daytime: 7-19h), E (evening: 19-23h), N (nighttime: 23-7h), and T (24-hour total).
  • Stations Catalog: 211346-4-estaciones-acusticas-csv.csv — contains station metadata including station ID, street address, neighborhood, district, and UTM coordinates.
R3. Open License for Republication Mandatory

Satisfied

The datasets are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license via datos.madrid.es. This permits copying, distribution, transformation, and online publishing for educational and commercial purposes with attribution.

R4. Linkable to Real-World Entities Mandatory

Satisfied

The station catalog enables direct linking to external knowledge graphs (Wikidata, DBpedia, GeoNames):

  • Geographical Entities: Station records contain district and neighborhood names (e.g., Centro → Wikidata wd:Q1763376, Justicia → Wikidata wd:Q1996570, and the city itself Madrid → Wikidata wd:Q2807).
  • Spatial Coordinates: UTM coordinates (X and Y columns) and geographic coordinates permit geospatial linking (WGS84 / GeoSPARQL).
  • Semantic Ontologies: Noise sensor readings map cleanly to standard ontologies such as SOSA/SSN (Sensor, Observation, Sample, and Actuation) and QUDT (Quantities, Units, Dimensions, and Types) using decibels (dB(A)).
R5. Documentation Exists Optional

Partially Satisfied

Official technical documentation (PDF format) is provided on the portal defining the 11 schema columns and statistical percentiles (L1, L10, L50, L90, L99, LAeq). The time interval definitions for periods D, E, N, and T are standardized under Spanish acoustic regulation (Real Decreto 1367/2007).

R6. Multiple Data Sources Optional

Satisfied

We integrate an additional open dataset from Madrid's data portal: the “Censo de locales, sus actividades y terrazas de hostelería y restauración” (Census of premises, activities, and hospitality terraces). (Number 200085) This dataset supplies street locations and authorized chair/table counts for outdoor terraces, which will be cross-referenced with nighttime acoustic noise (Periodo = N).

Data Preprocessing & Cleansing Steps

  • Identifier Reconciliation: Daily measurements identify stations via numeric IDs (2, 3...), while the station catalog uses labels like RF-02, RF-03. We map {n} → RF-0{n} during preprocessing.
  • Coordinate Correction: Geographic coordinates in the catalog use period separators erroneously (e.g., -3.691.877 instead of -3.691877). We rely on the correctly formatted UTM (X, Y) coordinates and project them to WGS84.
  • Missing Values & Numbers: Missing entries flagged as N/D will be mapped to null triples, and European decimal commas (68,9) are converted to standard floating-point numbers (68.9).
  • Subset Selection: Because the raw daily noise history spans from 1998 to present (several gigabytes), our repository includes a scoped extract focused on the most recent full year (2024/2025).
